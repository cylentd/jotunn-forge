    Guide gap

    Six-god priority lists can't be followed in a three-god run. The god boards rank picks within each god instead.

    Confirmed

    Four actives carry the damage: Ball Lightning, Iron Fist, Magnetized Anvil, Mjolnir. Mjolnir took a 13% share evolved at 29 min and 21% evolved at 14 min, so evolve order is the biggest lever.

    Open

    Arctic Arrow is crowd control (100% freeze, 3 s). The game logs damage, not freezes. 331 damage in 27 minutes is ~10 hits; a freeze needs a hit. Test: Skadi · Thor · Odin on Hard, Arctic Arrow as the only god active until minute 10. Under 1,000 damage = the arrows are not landing.

    Some bosses are freeze-immune. The Construct's final-phase rays linger 1 s after the red zones vanish, worst on Nightmare.

    Open

    Valkyrie Descent on bosses. Rank 3, never evolved, 100% slow. The logs can't split damage by target, so its boss damage is unmeasured.

    Pattern

    The boss decides the run. Zero damage taken in minutes 10–20 of both runs; 71% of Insane damage taken came in the last 4 minutes.

    Next test

    Nightmare 2 (310%) with Thor · Freya · Brokk and the Insane run's picks. Record damage taken in the last 4 minutes and the minute the 4th active evolves.
